The market

DeSci isn't just biology.
It's every field.

Existing DeSci platforms are locked to longevity and biotech. ETHesis is domain-agnostic by design — any area where research produces verifiable outputs can run on the platform.

Already in the pipeline

Machine Learning

Several independent ML researchers have expressed interest in launching ventures on ETHesis. Model weights, benchmark results, and code commits are machine-verifiable outputs — a natural fit for the agent.

Underserved today

Social Sciences & Economics

Academic papers, datasets, and replication studies. Fields where grant access is hardest and institutional gatekeeping is most severe. ETHesis removes the middleman.

Natural home

Crypto & Protocol Research

Security audits, cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, L2 design. Companies in this space already fund research but have no standardized accountability layer. ETHesis provides one.

Target partners: large foundations and companies that already distribute research grants — Mozilla, Protocol Labs, Ethereum Foundation — are natural anchors. We are in early conversations with grant program leads at two of them.
